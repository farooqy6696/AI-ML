Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly transforming the software industry. Modern
applications increasingly rely on machine learning models to automate tasks such as
document summarization, question generation, sentiment analysis, and natural language
understanding.

Large Language Models (LLMs) like Google's Gemini are capable of reading unstructured
text and producing structured, human-quality output. Enterprises use these models to
speed up recruitment, generate documentation, and build intelligent assistants that
integrate directly into existing business workflows.

Building production-grade AI systems requires more than just calling a model API. It
requires clean, layered architecture that separates concerns: data access (reading
files and talking to databases), business logic (calling AI services and processing
results), and presentation (the entry point that orchestrates everything and reports
status to the user). This separation makes systems easier to test, maintain, and
extend over time.
